## 关键 Claim 摘录

Claim 6:
> computing the NAV using a **delta calculation approach** that is based on a **contribution** of the financial instrument price information to the NAV.

Claim 7:
> adding the contribution to the retrieved **previous value** to thereby compute the NAV.

Claim 10–11:
> simultaneously computing a plurality of different NAVs … **bid NAV, an ask NAV, and a last NAV**.

Claim 16:
> calculating a **price delta** … generating a plurality of **delta events** … each delta event comprising the calculated price delta, a retrieved basket identifier, and a retrieved weight value.

Claim 20:
> storing a plurality of **pointers in a second table**, the pointers being indexed by a financial instrument identifier … pairs in the first table are indexed by the pointers.

Claim 34:
> the coprocessor comprises a **reconfigurable logic device** … **field programmable gate array (FPGA)**.

Claim 24–28:
> processing the NAV against a **triggering condition** … comparing **dissimilarity** with trigger threshold … determine whether the NAV is to be reported.

## 对本报告的支撑点

- **[事实]** 硬件 basket/NAV 增量路径（Δ contribution + previous value）有公开专利背书，与本报告 B0「partial[leg]+=qty×Δpx」同构。
- **[事实]** symbol 反向索引 + basket 正向表的双表结构有 Claim 20 直接描述。
- **[事实]** bid/ask/last 多路 NAV 并行计算有 Claim 11 描述 — 对应可选第二/三路累加器。
- **[事实]** 对外 dissemination 可与片内逐笔计算解耦（threshold gate），Claim 24–28。
- **[推断]** 专利未声称具体 ns 延迟 — 本报告 **禁止写死 cycle/ns**。
